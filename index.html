A familiar form, reimagined

Rooted in the mountains.
At home anywhere.

Inspired by the honest utility of an Appalachian tobacco sorting table, this concept gives a working form a more sculptural life. Warm walnut, softly rounded edges, and open trestle frames make room for the day to unfold—from a morning at your desk to a long evening around the table.
